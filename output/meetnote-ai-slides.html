MeetNote AI02 / 05

The problem with manual meeting notes

Details get missed during discussion

Students have to listen, contribute and take notes at the same time.

Recordings take time to review

Finding a decision, task owner or deadline often means replaying the audio.

Our objective

Give student project teams an editable transcript and meeting notes they can share.

MeetNote AI03 / 05

How MeetNote AI works

  1. 01Record or import
  2. 02Transcribe
  3. 03Review and edit
  4. 04Generate notes
  5. 05Export
Whisper Tiny
Converts audio to text on the CPU using faster-whisper.
Qwen3 4B
Turns the reviewed transcript into notes and a mind map.
Ollama
Runs Qwen3 on the same computer, keeping the transcript local.

Export the transcript and notes as Word or plain text.

Challenges & lessons learned

ChallengeWhat we learned
Live transcription lag

Measure the delay while someone is speaking.

Unexpected words from another language

Test recordings one language at a time.

Misheard names

Correct names before generating notes so each task goes to the right person.
